Concept 01 • Monogram With Meaning
The "Taste & Service" Monogram S
Dual fluid ribbons forming an iconic S — rising aroma (Swaad) meeting a serving katori bowl (Sevak).
Design Rationale: Built from two interlocking, mathematically harmonized continuous ribbons. The top curl evokes rising culinary steam and rich flavor (Swaad), while the bottom sweep forms the cradling curve of a hospitality bowl or waiter's serving hand (Sevak). An angled 45° ticket notch in negative space signals live order flow. Reads instantly as a confident "S" at tiny favicon sizes.

Concept 02 • Steam / Chai-Cup Mark
The Geometric Kulhad & Steam
Tapered terracotta kulhad silhouette with rising dual S-curve steam trails.
Design Rationale: Anchored in India's quintessential chai and hospitality vessel: the terracotta kulhad. Rebuilt with minimal geometric lines, the cup has a tapered body, while two rising aromatic steam ribbons echo the soft curvature of the letter "S". Instantly communicates café, street food, and warm culinary service.

Concept 03 • Service + Tech Mark
The Diya Flame & Service Bell
Serving cloche dome crowned by an auspicious diya flame and digital sync dot.
Design Rationale: Merges the universal symbol of restaurant hospitality (the service cloche / banquet bell) with the traditional Indian diya flame. A subtle circular beacon on the upper rim denotes live cloud connectivity (real-time KDS orders, table QR alerts). Clean geometric arcs make it bold and architectural.

Concept 04 • Wordmark-Led Identity
The Signature "Kadai" Wordmark & Seal
Custom sculpted wordmark paired with an interlocking geometric seal stamp.
Design Rationale: Puts the strong phonetic identity of Swaad Sevak at center stage. The initial "S" is sculpted with a continuous ladle/serving arc, while the twin "aa" characters are joined by an unbroken hospitality ligature. Paired with a geometric rotated diamond stamp with interlocking curves for compact icon applications.
